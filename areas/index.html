You drive to us.

The wash is at 95 High Street, Clapham. Distances below are roughly how far each place is from the bay, as the crow flies. The road can be a little longer.

Clapham

on the High Street

The bay is at 95 High Street, in the row with the motor trade units. Look for the black front and the Clapham Car Care sign.

Brickhill

around 1 mile

Brickhill sits just south of the village. It is a short run up towards Clapham High Street.

Oakley

around 2 miles

Oakley is the next village north. Come down through Clapham and the wash is on the High Street.

Bromham

around 2 miles

Bromham is to the west. Drivers come across and leave the car with us for a Level 1 or a booked extra.

Biddenham

around 2 miles

Biddenham is south-west of the bay. The High Street unit is easy to find once you are in the village.

Milton Ernest

around 2 miles

Milton Ernest is north of Oakley. People drive down to the bay rather than using a supermarket wash.

Bedford

around 2 miles

Bedford town centre is south of Clapham. Come up the High Street. We are open seven days, with shorter hours on Sunday.

Putnoe

around 3 miles

Putnoe is on the north side of Bedford. The drive up to Clapham is straightforward in normal traffic.

Goldington

around 3 miles

Goldington is east of Bedford town. Allow a little longer if you are coming at school-run time.

Ravensden

around 3 miles

Ravensden is north-east of Bedford. The bay is a village hand wash, not a drive-through tunnel.

Kempston

around 3 miles

Kempston is south of the river. Drivers bring daily cars and larger vehicles. A large car, van, or 4x4 is £19 for Level 1.

Renhold

around 4 miles

Renhold is east of the town. Call before you set off if you want an extra such as paint correction, so we can book the time.

If your village is not on this list, call 07530 077007. We will tell you if the drive makes sense. We do not run a mobile wash.
