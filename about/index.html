A hand wash with detailing habits.

Clapham Car Care works from a bay on the High Street in Clapham, the village just north of Bedford.

The Google profile describes a car cleaning and detailing service for Clapham in north Bedfordshire. The work runs from a maintenance wash through to paint correction, and scratch and dent repair.

The bay was set up to use proper detailing methods, with the ease of a normal hand car wash. You drive in. You do not need an account or an app.

Customers describe the team as family-run. The owner replies on Google as the Clapham Car Care Team. The sign on the unit also carries the name TNL The Next Level Automotive.

One car or a fleet, the profile says they can take the booking. Cash and card are both accepted. Prices for extra work are agreed before the job starts.
